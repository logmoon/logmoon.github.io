    Stock Boy is a 2D action-adventure side-scrolling game I developed using the Unity game engine. This project marked my first experience working with a team, where I took on the role of lead developer and was the sole in-engine programmer. It was an incredible learning experience, and I had a blast bringing the game to life.

    Although I can't disclose too much about the game's details, as it's not my intellectual property, I can say that working on this project was a major milestone for me. It pushed me to learn new skills, collaborate effectively with others, and manage the complexities of a larger-scale project. This experience has definitely shaped the way I approach future projects.
